Link converter

Paste an X (Twitter) post or any web/news article URL. X links become xcancel.com, other URLs get a Wayback Machine link, and already-mirrored links unwrap back to the original.

Open

X hosts (x.com, twitter.com, mobile.twitter.com, m.x.com) are rewritten to xcancel.com with tracking params (s, t, ref_src, ref_url, cxt) stripped.

Other URLs are wrapped with https://web.archive.org/web/ (or /save/, /web/*/).

Mirrored URLs unwrap: paste a web.archive.org/web/… or xcancel.com link to get the original back.

Bookmarklet: drag Convert this page to your bookmarks bar to convert whatever page you're on.

Tip: link to ?u=<url> to pre-fill the input.
